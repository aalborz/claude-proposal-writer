## 1. Executive Summary

Harborview's front desk staff spend a large part of the day answering repetitive calls and messages: "What are your hours?", "How much does a visit cost?", "Do you have an opening for [service]?" During busy hours, calls go unanswered and the patient calls a different clinic instead.

We'll design and launch a smart assistant that answers patients' frequently asked questions 24/7, based only on the clinic's official information, guides them toward booking an appointment, and logs the requesting patient's information. Result: uninterrupted responsiveness, fewer lost patients during busy hours, and front-desk time freed up for patients who are physically at the clinic.

---

## 2. Our Understanding of Harborview's Situation

Through our conversations, the following became clear:

- A high volume of calls and messages are **repetitive**, and answering them takes a lot of the front desk's time.
- During **peak hours**, some calls go unanswered and the patient turns to another clinic.
- Most questions are predictable: **hours, address and directions, list of services and providers, approximate cost, and how to book an appointment.**
- In healthcare, **accuracy and trust** are critical; the assistant must never give medical advice or a diagnosis, and must refer clinical matters to a provider.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

A smart assistant that answers only from the clinic's official information (services, providers, hours, appointment terms) and never invents anything. This assistant:

- Answers patients' frequent questions in a respectful tone, **24/7**.
- Guides patients toward **booking an appointment** and logs their appointment request and contact information.
- **Safety boundary:** never gives medical advice, a diagnosis, or a prescription; refers clinical questions to a provider.
- Refers questions it doesn't know to a human staff member instead of guessing.
- Works across two channels: a **website chat widget** and **WhatsApp**, both from one shared brain.

Alongside it, an **admin panel** for the clinic: updating information and services, viewing conversations and appointment requests, and performance reporting.
