## 1. Executive Summary

Solstice Apparel's support team spends hours every day answering repetitive questions — "Where's my order?", "What's your return policy?", "Is this item in stock?" — and during evening and holiday hours, when site traffic peaks, these questions go unanswered and some purchases get abandoned partway through.

We'll design and launch a smart support assistant that answers over 80% of frequent questions 24/7, based only on the store's official information, looks up order status, and guides hesitant shoppers to complete their purchase. Result: uninterrupted responsiveness, fewer abandoned carts, and support time freed up for the cases that matter most.

---

## 2. Our Understanding of Solstice's Situation

Through our conversations, the following became clear:

- A high daily volume of **repetitive** questions comes through WhatsApp and Instagram DMs, taking a lot of the two-person support team's time.
- The heaviest purchase traffic is at **night and on holidays** — exactly when support isn't active; shoppers waiting on a reply often abandon the purchase.
- The most frequent questions are predictable: **order status and tracking, shipping and return terms, stock availability, and sizing/product guidance.**
- Solstice's brand cares a lot about a warm, trustworthy tone; answers must be accurate and never give a customer wrong information.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

A smart assistant that answers only from Solstice's official content (store guide, FAQ, shipping and return policy) and never invents anything. This assistant:

- Answers common customer questions in Solstice's brand voice, **24/7**.
- Looks up **order status** using a tracking code and informs the customer.
- Guides a hesitant shopper to the right product with simple questions and accompanies them through checkout.
- Refers questions it doesn't know **to the human team** instead of guessing.
- Works across two channels: a **website chat widget** and **WhatsApp**, both from one shared brain.

Alongside it, an **admin panel** for Solstice: updating the knowledge base, viewing conversations and unanswered questions, and performance reporting.
